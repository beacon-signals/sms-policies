# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

## Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to a personal automated SMS notification system operated by Cully Guidry, the registered sole proprietor. The system sends transactional and operational text message alerts only to users who have explicitly consented to receive them.

## Information Collected

The system may use a mobile telephone number and information necessary to deliver operational SMS notifications.

The system does not collect information for advertising or marketing purposes.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to provide automated operational notifications, including:

- Program status and failure alerts
- Restart and recovery notifications
- End-of-day validation results
- Database backup and verification results
- Successful daily system completion notifications

## SMS Consent and Data Sharing

SMS consent and mobile telephone information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information necessary to deliver SMS messages may be processed by service providers used to operate the messaging service.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on system activity and operational events.

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opting Out

Recipients may reply STOP to discontinue SMS messages.

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the SMS notification system. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
